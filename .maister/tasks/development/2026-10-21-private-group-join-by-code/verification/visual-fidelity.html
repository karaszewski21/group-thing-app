TL;DR

Fidelity is high: all six mockup screens are present, with matching layout, field order, primary actions and Polish copy. The deviations are the documented AuthGateLinks copy ("Zarejestruj się" instead of "Załóż konto"), a missing "⧉" glyph on the copy buttons, and the stepper CTA, which is conditional and absent for an organizer who already has an organization. This report does not gate completion.

Open Questions / Risks

None. There is no substantive drift; the design owner may accept the missing glyph or ask for it to be restored.
