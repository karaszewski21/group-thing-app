TL;DR

  • POINTS becomes a third listing mode. The owner picks points_price within bounds around the item's stored points_value, served by GET /api/points/item-value?item_id= (owner-only) and enforced by the same function on PUT.
  • „Biorę” posts HOLD (buyer PARTY → buyer HELD). The buyer's confirm posts a 4-entry SALE (HELD −p, BURN +p, EMISSION −p, seller +p). Any cancel posts RELEASE. POINTS take and confirm need a login token; OAuth mcp:* tokens get 403.
  • The ledger moves to declared debit/credit types, a non-negative balance check and one account per (party, type), under one lock order: accounts → item → preference. Migration 0109 has a guarded downgrade.
  • points_price and held_points become the R8 exceptions. Eight binding screens are implemented, and /add retries only the PUT after a failure.

Open Questions / Risks

  • acceptedThe pair limit is not strict across GIFT and POINTS. A GIFT confirm can take a slot an open hold reserved. The overshoot is at most the pair's open holds, and burn still equals emission.
  • acceptedShared products and the photo rule. If the product is full (10 photos) with none of them the owner's, the owner cannot list for points. If the owner uploaded a photo for an earlier item, the /add hint blocks while the server would accept; My Items or the edit form still work.
  • acceptedHeld points can stay locked indefinitely. The seller can cancel, and the 30-day reminder is deferred.
  • highThe ledger contract changes for the first time. PARTY debits become possible, with a non-negative check under the lock. The tests are rewritten deliberately.
  • noteEvery account read keyed by party_id must also filter account_type, including the stage-1 lock_accounts callers and the test helpers.
  • noteThe RFC 8693 bridge token carries EDIT, so it passes the login check, as it passes row 69 today. This is unchanged and recorded.
  • notePOINTS listings match wants with offer_mode NULL; run the matcher suite.
  • opsNo image-only rollback past 0109. An old image rejects re-saving a POINTS want.
  • noteThe stage-1 record ("frozen at listing", "badge without price") is superseded.

Scope

Goal: let people spend their points: owners list an item for an owner-set price within system bounds, buyers reserve it by holding that price, and the buyer's confirmation burns the held points while the seller receives the same amount, so the points in circulation do not change.

In scope
  • 4 config keys + enforced max_points_offers
  • Pure price domain; item-value in item_id and category forms
  • POINTS listing via PUT (photo rule, limit, M4 re-check, stored-value bounds)
  • HOLD at take, SALE at buyer confirm, RELEASE at any cancel
  • Login-token-only POINTS take/confirm
  • Ledger contract, per-type accounts, BURN/HELD, one lock order
  • points_price/held_points on read models; POINTS counts, search chip, wants
  • Migration 0109 + runbook; 8 binding screens; docs
Out of scope
  • Pickup condition correction and SALE_REFUND; the seller block after 3 corrections
  • The 30-day reminder; auto-confirm, objections, hold expiry
  • Points notifications beyond the „za N pkt” text
  • POINTS on POST /api/inventory-items; price on /details and term listings
  • Recomputing stored prices after config changes; unlisting on a lower cap
  • A strict pair limit across GIFT/SWAP and POINTS
  • Restricting mcp:edit on POINTS cancel or listing
  • START_BONUS, points at events, a payment gateway

User Stories

Owner — list an item „Za punkty” at a price I choose within the allowed range, to earn points for things I no longer need.
Owner — see the suggestion and the allowed range, to pick a fair price without guessing.
Buyer — see the price on cards, in search and on the item page, before opening it.
Buyer — see my available balance and what remains after reserving, to know whether I can afford it.
Buyer — have my points only held until I confirm the pickup, so I don't lose them if the hand-over fails.
Seller — receive the price when the buyer confirms, and be able to cancel (returning the hold), so a no-show never blocks my item.
Logged-in person — see available, held and reputation on /points, with labelled holds, sales, purchases and releases.
Admin — tune the ratios, cap, step and offer limit without a deploy.

R3.4 listing steps and messages
  1. Profile.
  2. Read the preference unlocked; becomes_points = no row or a non-POINTS row; if so, lock_owner_accounts → (owner, PARTY).
  3. Item FOR UPDATE, ownership (403), live-location rule.
  4. Re-read the preference; if becomes_points changed → 409 „Oferta zmieniła się w międzyczasie — odśwież.”
  5. NULL points_value → 400 „Tej rzeczy nie można wystawić za punkty — brak wyceny.”
  6. No own non-rejected photo → 400 „Dodaj przynajmniej jedno zdjęcie, aby wystawić za punkty.” (product_service.has_own_unrejected_photo via product_bridge)
  7. Only when becomes_points: count ≥ max_points_offers → 409 „Masz już maksymalną liczbę ofert za punkty ({n}).”
  8. Price against price_bounds(stored points_value): invalid → 400 „Cena musi być od {min} do {max} pkt, co {step}.”; omitted → keep the existing price, or clamp the suggestion.
  9. Write, set ACTIVE, commit. Steps 2–9 run in mapped_transaction_conflicts.

Non-POINTS modes NULL the price. points_price with another mode → 400 „Cena w punktach dotyczy tylko oferty za punkty.”

R5–R7 take, confirm, cancel messages
  • Missing price on a POINTS take → 400 „Podaj cenę w punktach.”; a price with another type → 400 „Cena w punktach dotyczy tylko oferty za punkty.”
  • An mcp:edit-only token on a POINTS take or confirm → 403 via require_login_principal (needs PERMISSION_EDIT).
  • Price mismatch → 409 „Cena zmieniła się — odśwież.”
  • Not eligible → 409 „Za punkty mogą kupować tylko osoby z kontem.”; family → 409 „Nie można kupować za punkty od członka własnej rodziny.”
  • Pair limit → 409 „Limit nagród dla tej pary został wyczerpany — spróbuj za kilka dni.”; balance → 409 „Za mało punktów — ktoś był szybszy albo masz inne blokady.”
  • SALE after RELEASE, or vice versa → 409 „Ta transakcja została już rozstrzygnięta”.
  • _require_exchange_legs message: „Wymiana to jedna rezerwacja GIFT albo POINTS albo dwie sparowane rezerwacje SWAP”.
  • Take notifications gain „ za {p} pkt”.
  • The item-value form error → 400 „Podaj item_id albo category_id i condition.”

Accounts, post_points, lock_accounts and reads
  • 0109 drops uq_point_accounts_party_id and adds uq_point_accounts_party_id_account_type. It recreates ck_point_accounts_party_id_account_type (PARTY/HELD with a party, EMISSION/BURN without), adds the partial uq_point_accounts_account_type_burn, and seeds BURN. All of this is mirrored in PointAccount.__table_args__.
  • post_points: entries are on the declared debit/credit types, sum to 0 and are non-zero; every debited PARTY/HELD account must stay ≥ 0 under the caller's lock, else LedgerInvariantError. It only flushes.
  • lock_accounts(keys: (party_id, type)): sorted INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, then FOR UPDATE ORDER BY party_id, account_type. Stage-1 sites change: awards.py:70/182 pass PARTY keys, :127/:202 index by tuple, _attendance_in_cap_month (:185, :208-223) takes the PARTY ids, and emission_account_id → system_account_id (:106/:188).
  • hold_amounts(reservation_ids) takes one query.
  • Reads: {balance, held, reputation}. History has one row per transaction: HOLD (−p / +p held), RELEASE (+p / −p held), SALE buyer (0 / −p held), SALE seller (+p / 0).

Lock order

one total order: point accounts (sorted) → item FOR UPDATE → preference PUT POINTS [(owner, PARTY) if becoming POINTS] → item → preference (re-checked) PATCH revalue item → preference (update_item wrapped) confirm accounts (award / SALE) → items by id → preference delete → fulfil cancel accounts (first in _release) → reservation / balance rows event delete reservations FOR UPDATE → all buyers' accounts once, sorted (wrapped) take (exc.) event FOR SHARE → preference FOR UPDATE → accounts → reservation rows locks no item row; PUT's item is verified non-POINTS, and confirm's item is reserved, so neither can close a cycle with take

Every path above runs in mapped_transaction_conflicts, so a residual deadlock is a 409, never a 500.

Migration 0109

Upgrade (lock_timeout 5s)
  1. 4 config columns NOT NULL with temporary server defaults, then the defaults dropped
  2. points_price SMALLINT + 2 CHECKs
  3. The account constraints + the BURN index
  4. Insert the BURN row
Downgrade (guarded)

Refuses while any of these counts is non-zero: HOLD/SALE/RELEASE transactions, POINTS preferences, POINTS reservations; the recovery is the pre-0109 dump. Otherwise it deletes HELD/BURN, restores the constraints and drops the column and config columns.

Runbook: pg_dump first, the matcher suite, backend before web, no image-only rollback, and an old image rejects re-saving a POINTS want.

Technical approach
  • Boundaries: groups points_acl gains lock_owner_accounts, price_bounds, offer_rules, lock_purchase, post_hold and hold_amounts. Inventory points_acl gains release_hold and lock_release_accounts. Points inventory_acl gains the owned-item value read. _POINTS_IMPORTERS is unchanged.
  • Login-token rule: require_login_principal raises 403 unless the principal holds PERMISSION_EDIT. It is called for a POINTS take (before any read) and a POINTS confirm (after resolve).
  • Data flow: form (bounds from item-value) → PUT → read models → sheet with /api/points/me → HOLD → hand-over → SALE or RELEASE → history.
  • Clock: naive-UTC ledger timestamps, no new clock reads.

Success criteria

  • s = 40 → 20–60, s = 60 → 30–90, s = 20 → 10–30, never above 100; 55 rejected with „Cena musi być od 20 do 60 pkt, co 10.”
  • For an existing item, the edit form and PUT share the stored-value bounds, even after a config change.
  • No own photo → 400. The 6th offer → 409, also under the M4 race.
  • A take holds exactly the price; each failure is a 409 and writes nothing.
  • An mcp:edit-only token gets 403 on a POINTS take and confirm.
  • Only the buyer confirms, and the seller sees no confirm button. SALE burn = emission = the seller's credit, with no reputation. Cancel releases, and SALE and RELEASE never both happen.
  • A deadlock on PUT, PATCH, confirm, cancel or event deletion is a 409, never a 500.
  • /add never creates a second item after a failed POINTS PUT.
  • /points shows available/held/reputation and the history. The read models show points_price/held_points only for POINTS and their parties.
  • 0109 round-trips and refuses with POINTS data. All tests and the matcher suite pass; mypy/ruff stay at 15/65 and the frontend at the 33 pre-existing failures.
