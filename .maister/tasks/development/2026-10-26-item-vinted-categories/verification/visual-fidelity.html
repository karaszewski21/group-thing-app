TL;DR

  • High structural fidelity: 7 ✓, 4 ⚠, 0 ✗.
  • The user-facing flows (add item, three buttons, picker browse and search, gift-limit error) match closely.
  • The deviations are admin styling (Chakra blue, text-style actions) plus two layout details: no visible "Nazwa" label in the edit editor, and the needed-items add row collapsed behind a button.

Key Decisions

  • Mockups were rendered at the same viewports as the app captures (390×844 user, 1366×900 admin); the gallery chrome and annotations were ignored.
  • Component IDs were judged through their host screens.

Open Questions / Risks

  • Should the admin screens adopt the mockup's green palette, or keep the existing Chakra admin theme?
