TL;DR

Fidelity is high. Layout order, copy and states match the binding mockups, including the intended overrides (M3 footer, gender-neutral copy). The deviations are the merge-card divider placement and attendee-list item rows that could not be checked with local data. Shared: GroupHeader stacks back/eyebrow/h1 instead of one row (reused as-is per spec), and the page sits inside the app shell.

Open Questions / Risks

  • Item rows, "Pokaż wszystkie (n)" and the banner were never rendered; their fidelity is unverified.
  • The app shell (search bar + bottom nav, also for anonymous visitors) is not in the mockups. Confirm that this is intended.
